Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Clipper Studio ("we", "our", or "the App") is an internal tool designed to help content creators automatically upload short-form video clips to social media platforms including TikTok and YouTube. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Clipper Studio, we may collect and process the following types of data:

  • Video files — MP4 clips generated from livestream recordings, used solely for uploading to your social media accounts.
  • Account credentials (OAuth tokens) — Access tokens and refresh tokens for TikTok and YouTube, stored locally on your device. We never transmit these tokens to third-party servers.
  • Publication metadata — Video titles, descriptions, tags, and publication timestamps stored in a local SQLite database.
  • TikTok account information — Basic account info (username, user ID) obtained via the TikTok API after you grant authorization, used only to identify which account to publish to.

3. How We Use Your Information

All collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Upload video clips to your TikTok and YouTube accounts on your behalf.
  • Track publication status of uploaded clips (published, scheduled, or pending).
  • Display analytics (views, likes, comments) from platform APIs in the app dashboard.

We do not sell, share, rent, or disclose your data to any third party for any purpose.

4. Data Storage

All data — including OAuth tokens, video files, and metadata — is stored locally on your own computer. No data is sent to or stored on any external servers operated by us. The only external communication is directly with the TikTok API and YouTube API on your behalf.

5. TikTok API Data Usage

Clipper Studio uses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to publish video content to accounts that have explicitly authorized the application. We access only the permissions required for video publishing (video.publish, user.info.basic). We do not access your followers, messages, or any private account data beyond what is necessary for publishing.

Data obtained through TikTok APIs is not shared with any third parties and is used only within the application for the purpose described above. We comply with TikTok's Privacy Policy and platform terms.

6. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored until you manually revoke access or delete the token files from your device. Publication records are kept in the local database indefinitely unless you delete them through the app. You can revoke the app's access to your TikTok account at any time via TikTok Settings → Security → Authorized Apps.

7. Your Rights

As a user of this application, you have the right to:

  • Revoke the app's access to your social media accounts at any time.
  • Delete all locally stored data by removing the application and its database files.
  • Request information about what data is stored by contacting us.

8. Security

Since all data is stored locally, security depends on your device's own protections. We recommend keeping your device secure and not sharing the credentials folder with others. OAuth tokens are stored in JSON files on your local filesystem.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
